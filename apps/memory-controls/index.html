What Spotify remembers about you

Something wrong? Correct it

Your correction wins. The old version is kept as history, never silently overwritten.

Pause memory

Stops Spotify using your memories to personalise answers on this surface. Nothing is deleted — your memories stay, they are just not used.

Opt out of memory

Tells Spotify to stop collecting new memories for your account. This sends a recorded opt-out event, so the decision is traceable.

Deletion progress

Every place your memory was stored reports separately, so a partial failure is visible.
